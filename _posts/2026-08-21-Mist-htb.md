> **TL;DR**
> Insane Windows AD box.
> A file-disclosure bug in Pluck CMS leaked the adminpassword, and a malicious module upload gave a webshell as svc_web.
> overwriting a.lnk in a writable directory phished a domain user, and with LDAP signing off,PetitPotam → ntlmrelayx relayed to the DC for a shadow credential on MS01$.
> Machineaccess gave local admin, a KeePass DB cracked with hashcat gave a domain account.
> A GMSA read → AddKeyCredentialLink → double ADCS ESC13 chain ended as Domain Admin.
